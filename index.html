What I Do

Services

Nothing exotic, just solid work done properly. Here's what I offer.

The Usual Basic Exterior

Hand wash, windows, and a spray sealant finish. A solid maintenance wash that keeps your vehicle looking sharp between full details.

Exterior

Trail Ready Full Exterior

Hand wash, deep wheel and tire clean, glass, and a ceramic sealant finish. Any sun-faded plastic trim is restored to a deep, dark finish. Every panel gets proper attention.

Exterior

Mud Season Basic Interior

Vacuum throughout, wipe-down of all surfaces, and window cleaning. A quick, clean refresh that makes the cabin feel cared for again.

Interior

Date Night Full Interior

Deep vacuum, full surface wipe-down, carpet and upholstery extraction, door jambs, and interior dressing. A complete reset from top to bottom.

Interior

Showstopper Full Detail

The complete interior and exterior treatment together. Best value if you want the whole vehicle done right in one appointment.
